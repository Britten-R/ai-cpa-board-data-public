MACPA Report to the Board
Rebekah Olson provided an update on recent and upcoming activities of the Maryland Association of CPAs (MACPA).

  • Student Leadership Academy: The MACPA Student Leadership Academy was held at the University of Baltimore and was a successful event, with more than 30 students representing 18 colleges from across Maryland. There was significant interest and excitement among students regarding the new CPA pathway recently established in Maryland.
  • Ethics Course Requirements: Ms. Olson reported that MACPA has received an increased number of questions regarding the ethics course requirements following the July 1 deadline. Under the current requirements, applicants can no longer rely on the previous grandfather provisions and must complete an ethics course specifically focused on business or accounting ethics. Ms. Olson noted that the Maryland Board of Accountancy website may not yet fully reflect the updated requirements, which has created some confusion. MACPA is also updating its website resources regarding the licensure process and plans to have those materials updated by the October 1 deadline.
  • AICPA/NASBA CPE Task Force: Ms. Olson briefed the Board on the joint AICPA and NASBA CPE Task Force established earlier this year to evaluate the current continuing professional education (CPE) framework and determine whether it continues to meet the needs of the evolving accounting profession. The task force is examining the knowledge and capabilities CPAs need throughout their careers, as well as how model regulations can be better aligned with the profession and consistently enforced and measured, with public protection remaining the primary objective. A third-party research firm is assisting with the project.

Two surveys are expected to be launched in the coming weeks. One survey will seek input from CPAs, with NASBA working with state boards to distribute it, while the second will solicit feedback from regulators and other stakeholders, including business users of financial information. The task force is expected to issue an exposure draft by the end of the year.

  • October 6 Board Meeting: Ms. Olson expressed enthusiasm about the upcoming October 6 meeting at Howard Community College and the opportunity for the CPA Board and MACPA Foundation Board to meet concurrently and then engage with students. She noted that MACPA is pleased to provide an opportunity for Board members from both organizations to interact and participate in a student-focused event following the meetings.
  • CPA Swearing-In Ceremony: Ms. Olson announced that the next CPA Swearing-In Ceremony is scheduled for November 18. Invitations are expected to be distributed shortly to newly licensed CPAs.

Ms. Olson concluded by noting that there are many positive developments underway for the accounting profession and expressed enthusiasm about the continued progress and upcoming activities.
