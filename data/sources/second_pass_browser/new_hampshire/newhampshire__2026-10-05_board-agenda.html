  9. IV.

    Welcome NASBA - Kent Absec - VP, State Board Relations - Dan Vuckovich - NASBA Mountain Regional Director - APS and PE Conversation - 10AM

    1.             What, if any legislative changes should be considered to ensure that we as a board have adequate authority to oversee CPA firms operating within APS structures?

     

    2.             Which issues are best addressed through legislation, Board rules, licensure or renewal requirements, or other processes?

     

    3.             Are there model provisions or statutory language that NASBA would recommend considering?

     

    4.             What additional information gathering tools we should consider to effectively regulate these structures?

     

    5.             If the existing AICPA Code of Professional Conduct adequately addresses the complexities created by PE-backed APS arrangements. Should a different or supplemental code of conduct, specific independence requirements, or other professional standards be considered?

     

    6.             Should certain disclosures be required so that clients and the public clearly understand which entity is the licensed CPA firm, which entity performs non-attest services, and how the entities are related?

     

    7.             If CPA firms should be required to disclose APS relationships, private equity investment, shared services arrangements, etc. as part of licensure / renewal?

     

    8.             What approaches have other jurisdictions considered that effectively fulfil their public protection role and promote meaningful transparency without creating an undue burden on firms?

     

    9.             Are there any additional measures to consider to promote effective peer review and quality oversight?

     

    10.       What information should the board seek to obtain on an ongoing basis to identify and address these potential risks before they result in harm to the public?
